PAST MEETING · MATERIALS AVAILABLE
AUG 28, 2026
Build a REST API in an hour
Presented by Maya Rivera · Holmes Hall 112 · 6:00 – 7:30 PM · 31 attended
Download slides
Open repository
We built a small Express API from an empty folder — routes, JSON responses, a Postgres connection, and enough error handling to not be embarrassing — then called it from a one-file frontend.
The session assumed you had written some JavaScript before but nothing else. If you missed it, the recording plus the starter and finished repositories are enough to follow along on your own; the cheat sheet at the bottom is the one-page version people asked for afterwards.
What we covered
01What REST actually means, and why we say GET, POST, PATCH, DELETE
02Setting up Express and returning your first JSON response
03Connecting Postgres and writing two queries without an ORM
04Testing endpoints with curl and Postman
05Status codes, validation, and where errors should live

How the team works
One 45-minute standup a week after the general meeting, plus whatever you do on your own time — most contributors spend two to four hours a week. Work is tracked as GitHub issues, every change goes through a pull request, and the lead or mentor reviews it with you rather than at you. Nothing is graded and nobody is dropped for a slow week.
